# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people, and keep the space welcoming to contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at yoad.elkayam@gmail.com. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.
